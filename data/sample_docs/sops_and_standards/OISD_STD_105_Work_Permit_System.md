# OISD-STD-105: Work Permit System in Hydrocarbon Industry
## 1. Scope and Objective
This standard covers the essential requirements of the Work Permit System to ensure that maintenance, inspection, and construction work in the hydrocarbon processing and handling installations are carried out safely without causing any hazard to personnel, equipment, or environment.

## 2. Types of Work Permits
1. **Cold Work Permit (Form A - Blue)**: For general maintenance, visual inspection, painting, non-sparking tool usage.
2. **Hot Work Permit (Form B - Pink/Red)**: Mandatory for welding, burning, grinding, open flame, vehicle entry in hazardous areas, or any operation generating heat/sparks.
3. **Confined Space Entry Permit (Form C - Yellow)**: Mandatory for entry into tanks, vessels, columns, pits, sewers, or excavations exceeding 1.2 meters depth.
4. **Electrical Isolation / Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) Permit (Form D - Green)**.

## 3. Mandatory Gas Testing Protocols for Hot Work & Confined Space
* **Hydrocarbon Gas Concentration (LEL)**: Must be strictly **0.0% LEL** prior to hot work. Permissible maximum for cold work is < 5% LEL.
* **Oxygen Content**: Must be between **19.5% and 23.5% vol/vol** for human entry.
* **Toxic Gases**:
  * Hydrogen Sulfide ($): Must not exceed **5.0 ppm** TWA (Threshold Limit Value). Evacuate immediately if $> 10.0 ppm$.
  * Carbon Monoxide ($): Must not exceed **25.0 ppm**.
  * Sulfur Dioxide ($): Must not exceed **2.0 ppm**.

## 4. Validity and Revalidation Matrix
* Permits are valid for a single shift (maximum 8 hours).
* Revalidation requires fresh atmospheric gas testing by the Area Safety Officer.
* Spark containment booths, continuous fire water pressurized hoses, and dry chemical powder (DCP) fire extinguishers must be physically positioned within 5 meters of hot work.
