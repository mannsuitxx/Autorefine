# API-510: Pressure Vessel Inspection Code: In-service Inspection, Rating, Repair, and Alteration

## 1. Scope and Application
This inspection code covers the in-service inspection, repair, alteration, and rerating activities for pressure vessels and the pressure-relieving devices protecting these vessels. It applies to hydrocarbon and chemical process vessels in refinery operating units.

## 2. Corrosion Rate Determination
The corrosion rate shall be calculated using the following formula:
Corrosion Rate (CR) = (t_previous - t_actual) / Time_years
Where:
- t_previous = thickness measured at the same CML during the previous inspection (in mm)
- t_actual = thickness measured during the current inspection (in mm)
- Time_years = time span between the two measurements (in years)

## 3. Remaining Life Calculation
The remaining life (RL) of the vessel component shall be calculated from:
Remaining Life (RL) = (t_actual - t_required) / Corrosion Rate
Where:
- t_required = minimum required design thickness including allowances (in mm)
- Corrosion Rate = calculated corrosion rate (in mm/year)

## 4. Maximum Allowable Inspection Intervals
- The maximum interval between internal or on-stream inspections shall not exceed one-half of the remaining life or 10 years, whichever is lesser.
- When the remaining life is less than 4 years, the inspection interval may equal the full remaining life up to a maximum of 2 years.
- When remaining life is less than 2 years, immediate engineering evaluation and repair authorization (e.g., internal weld overlay cladding or replacement) is mandatory prior to resuming operating cycles.

## 5. Weld Overlay Repairs and Alterations
- Weld overlay restoration shall utilize ASME Section IX qualified weld procedures and certified corrosion-resistant alloys (e.g., 316L SS or Inconel).
- Pre-heating and non-destructive examination (NDE) including 100% UTM and dye penetrant testing must be performed post-repair.
