# MRPL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: ULTRASONIC THICKNESS GAUGING
**Document Code**: MRPL-SOP-UTM-01
**Department**: Inspection & Asset Integrity Management

## 1. Scope & Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory guidelines for digital ultrasonic thickness measurement (UTM) across static equipment, process columns, drums, heat exchangers, and piping lines at MRPL Mangalore Refinery.

## 2. Equipment & Calibration Procedure
1. **Instrument Calibration**: Ultrasonic thickness meter with dual crystal 5.0 MHz or 7.5 MHz high-temperature transducer.
2. **Calibration Standard**: Step wedge calibration block (carbon steel SA-516 Gr 70 / ASTM A106 Gr B).
3. **Calibration Check**: Perform two-point calibration on step wedge prior to inspection and a zero check after every 25 readings.
4. **Couplant**: High-temperature silicone or carboxymethyl cellulose couplant applied uniformly.

## 3. Grid Mapping & Thickness Measurement Protocol
1. **Surface Preparation**: Wire brush or grit blast measurement area to SA 2.5 cleanliness. Remove loose paint, scale, and rust.
2. **Measurement Locations (TMLs)**: Record 4 quadrant readings (North, East, South, West) at each shell course, nozzle neck, and bottom head knuckle.
3. **Temperature Compensation**: Apply thickness compensation of +1% per 55°C for surface temperatures exceeding 60°C.
4. **Defect Threshold**: Any thickness reading below nominal minus corrosion allowance shall be flagged immediately for secondary scanning with angle beam shear wave.

## 4. Documentation & Reporting
1. All UTM data shall be signed by an ASNT/ISNT Level-II certified NDT inspector.
2. Thickness logs must report minimum thickness, nominal thickness, retirement limit (t_min), and calculate corrosion rate against baseline records.
