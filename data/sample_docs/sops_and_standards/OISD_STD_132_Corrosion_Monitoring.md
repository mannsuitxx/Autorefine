# OISD-STD-132: INSPECTION OF PRESSURE RELIEVING DEVICES & CORROSION MONITORING
**Standard**: Oil Industry Safety Directorate OISD-STD-132 / OISD-STD-105

## 1. Naphthenic Acid & High Temperature Sulfidation Corrosion
1. Crude distillation preheat exchangers (E-101, E-104) and vacuum furnace transfer lines are susceptible to naphthenic acid corrosion (NAC) and sulfidation at operating temperatures between 220°C and 400°C.
2. Mitigation requires metallurgy upgrade to SA-240 Type 316L or SA-213 TP317L with molybdenum content exceeding 2.5%.
3. Ammonium chloride salt deposition occurs in debutanizer and overhead accumulator reflux drums (V-101, V-205). Wash water injection rate must maintain 5% free water in overhead stream.

## 2. Decoking Safety & Thermal Cycling
1. Steam-air decoking of furnace coils mandates continuous skin thermocouple temperature monitoring.
2. Emergency quench water supply and nitrogen snuffing lines must be tested prior to decoking cycle.

## 3. Confined Space Entry Limits
1. Oxygen content must be between 19.5% and 23.5% by volume.
2. Hydrocarbon combustible gas (LEL) must be strictly 0.0%.
3. Toxic gas limits: H2S less than 10 ppm, Carbon Monoxide (CO) less than 25 ppm, Benzene less than 0.5 ppm.
