# MRPL Mangalore Refinery Standard Operating Procedure
## Document No: MRPL/SOP/HSE/2025/042-REV3
## Title: Safe Execution of Hot Work on Hydrocarbon Piping and Column Manways

### 1. Mandatory Pre-Conditions
1. The line or vessel must be positively isolated using spectacle blinds or slip blinds. Valve isolation alone is prohibited.
2. Steaming and nitrogen purging shall continue until volatile organic compound (VOC) levels are below 0.0 ppm.
3. Blind flange thickness must comply with ASME B16.48 ratings.
4. Continuous multi-gas monitor with calibrated $, $, and $ sensors must be actively worn by the designated safety observer.

### 2. Escalation & Approval Hierarchy
* Level 1 (Low Hazard): Area Maintenance Lead.
* Level 2 (Medium Hazard): Shift In-Charge & HSE Lead.
* Level 3 (High Hazard / Running Unit Proximity): Chief General Manager (Operations) & General Manager (HSE).
